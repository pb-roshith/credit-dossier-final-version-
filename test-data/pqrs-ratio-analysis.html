Management commentary

Fixed asset turnover improved from 4.05x in FY23 to 4.82x in FY26E as utilization increased without disproportionate asset additions. Capital expenditure remained controlled at approximately 4%–5% of sales.

Leverage reduced materially: total debt declined by INR 70 crore while EBITDA increased by INR 82.5 crore. Consequently, total debt to EBITDA improved from 3.04x to 1.30x. Lower borrowings and stronger earnings also raised interest coverage from 3.29x to 7.69x.

Profitability strengthened throughout the period. EBITDA margin expanded by 300 basis points to 15.00%, and net profit margin increased to 6.67%. Return on assets and return on equity improved to 10.12% and 24.85%, respectively, reflecting better utilization and retained earnings growth.

Credit interpretation and sensitivities

  • Leverage below 2.0x from FY25 indicates a strengthening solvency profile.
  • Interest coverage above 5.0x from FY25 provides an improved finance-cost cushion.
  • Liquidity improved, with the current ratio reaching 1.55x in FY26E.
  • Key sensitivities are raw-material price volatility, customer concentration, export demand, and execution risk on new capacity.
  • A sustained debt/EBITDA ratio above 2.5x or interest coverage below 3.0x would weaken the credit view.
